FAQ

Good to know

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2 pm and check-out is by 12 noon. Need an earlier arrival or a later departure? Just ask when you book.

How do I book direct?

Send us the villa name, your dates and the number of guests on WhatsApp. We'll confirm availability and the total price.

Can I book through travel apps?

Yes. Our properties are also listed on platforms such as Trip.com and tiket.com. For special requests or group bookings, WhatsApp is the quickest way.

What about payment, cancellations and events?

Terms vary by property and date. Our team will walk you through everything when you message us on WhatsApp.
